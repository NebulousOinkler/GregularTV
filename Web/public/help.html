Help

Gregular TV turns your Jellyfin library into always-on channels: shows and films on a schedule, with commercial breaks, a guide and channel surfing.

Connecting to your server

Enter your Jellyfin server's address, then sign in with a password or Quick Connect. Which addresses a browser can use:

  • https:// addresses work in every browser.
  • http:// addresses work only on your home network (such as 192.168.1.5:8096 or nas.local:8096), and only in Chrome or Edge, which ask to "look for devices on your local network". Allow it. Safari and Firefox don't allow it at all: use an https address there, for example through a reverse proxy or Tailscale.

Jellyfin lets any website connect by default. If your server, or a reverse proxy in front of it, limits which websites may connect (CORS), allow https://gregular.tv.

Watching

  • On a phone or tablet: tap the picture for the controls, and swipe across it to change channel. Every channel's now and next is under the picture.
  • With a keyboard: ← and → change channel, I shows what's on, L opens the channel list, S opens Settings, Space pauses (and again jumps back to live), Esc steps back to the guide and then your servers, and digits type a channel number.
  • Sound: browsers start videos silently until you've tapped or pressed a key. Tap "Tap for sound" if you see it.

If a programme won't play

Open Settings while it's on: "Trouble with…" offers a lower quality for that programme, which is quicker for your server to convert. Turning on "Show playback diagnostics" shows what the server is doing.

Your privacy

Nothing you watch, and nothing about your server, goes anywhere but your own browser and server. See Privacy.
