1. **Buffering on the real server, after the fixes of 2026-10-01.** Watching had become nearly impossible: frequent buffering, and subtitles often showing. Jellyfin was ignoring the app's "no subtitles" (it only honours a stream index for a request naming its media source) and could burn the default track in, a full re-encode; and Auto started at an 8 Mbps cap that a background speed test could lower further, making the Raspberry Pi re-encode any file above it. Now the request names its media source, Auto plays the original until there's trouble on the connection, and a re-encode makes H.264, not HEVC. Check on the Apple TV with *Show playback diagnostics* on: most programmes should say "no transcoding" or only a container or audio reason, no subtitles should show, and buffering should be rare. If it still buffers, note the diagnostics line when it does.

Reproduce with *Show playback diagnostics* on, and watch the deck state it shows.
